Superstore Profitability & Regional Performance Dashboard

Goal

Analyze sales and profitability across product categories, subcategories, and regions to identify areas driving strong performance and areas contributing to losses.

Description

The project evaluates Superstore profitability using KPIs, product and regional analysis, profit-versus-sales comparisons, Pareto analysis, outlier analysis, geographic analysis, and interactive filtering.

Skills

Dashboard design, KPI analysis, calculated fields, parameters, LOD expressions, Pareto analysis, geographic analysis, data visualization, business recommendations.

Technology

Tableau.

Results

Technology was the strongest-performing category, while Furniture showed weaker profitability. The West and East regions performed more strongly overall, while the Central region, particularly Texas, showed repeated losses across several Furniture subcategories.
